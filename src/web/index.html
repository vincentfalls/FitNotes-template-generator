📖 How to Restore on iOS & Android

1

Download the generated .fitnotes file

Click "Download .fitnotes Backup" and save it to your iPhone (e.g. iCloud Drive or Downloads).

2

Open the Files App on iPhone

Locate the downloaded .fitnotes file, tap and hold it, then tap Share.

3

Select FitNotes 2

Choose FitNotes 2 from the share sheet and tap Restore Database.

1

Download the generated .fitnotes file

Save the .fitnotes file onto your Android device or Google Drive.

2

Open FitNotes > Settings > Backup/Restore

In Android FitNotes, open the menu drawer, tap Settings, and navigate to Backup/Restore.

3

Tap Restore Backup

Select your downloaded .fitnotes file to restore your new routines instantly.
